# 🎮 Cipro - Simple Gaming, Real Rewards

**Play games. Complete tasks. Earn cryptocurrency.**

## What is Cipro?

Cipro is a **straightforward gaming platform** where you:
1. **Play 4 fun games** (Puzzle, Memory, Trivia, Spin Wheel)
2. **Complete daily tasks** to earn points
3. **Convert points to real crypto** (USDT, SOL, ETH)
4. **Withdraw to your wallet** when you reach minimum thresholds

**No complex systems. No confusing features. Just play and earn.**

## 🎯 How It Works (Simple 3-Step Process)

### Step 1: Play & Earn Points
- **Games**: 4 different games, each gives 20-100 points per play
- **Tasks**: Daily challenges for bonus points
- **Daily Login**: Get free points just for showing up

### Step 2: Track Your Progress  
- **Wallet**: See your total points and crypto value
- **Leaderboard**: Compare with other players
- **Achievements**: Unlock milestones for bonus rewards

### Step 3: Cash Out
- **Convert**: 10,000 points = $1 USD worth of crypto
- **Withdraw**: Minimum $5 withdrawal to your crypto wallet
- **Supported**: USDT, SOL, ETH, USDC
